🚨 If you think you have been scammed

Contact your bank or payment app through its official channel immediately. In India, call the cybercrime helpline 1930 promptly and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Save transaction IDs, messages and screenshots. Never pay someone who promises to “recover” your money for a fee.

❌ Red flags

Requests for PIN/OTP, “receive money” QR codes, remote-access apps, urgent threats, unknown collect requests.

✅ Safer actions

Stop, independently verify, open your payment app yourself, and reject unexpected requests.
